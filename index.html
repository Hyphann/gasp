Safety & How It Works

Important Safety Notice: Always practice sitting or lying down in a safe environment. Never practice in or near water (bath, pool, ocean), while driving, or standing up. Lightheadedness and tingling in hands or feet are normal. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

The 3 Phases of Each Round

  1. Power Breathing (30–40 breaths): Inhale deeply through the nose or mouth, filling your belly and chest, then simply let go without forcing the exhale.
  2. Retention (Exhale Hold): After the final natural exhale, hold your breath until you feel the urge to breathe, then tap Breathe In.
  3. Recovery Inhale & Hold: Take a deep, full breath in at your own pace, then tap Start 15s Hold once your lungs are full.

Install on iPad Home Screen

In Safari on your iPad, tap the Share icon in the toolbar and choose Add to Home Screen. Hyphan Breath will launch full-screen and work completely offline. (Make sure iPad Silent Mode is off if you don't hear audio.)
